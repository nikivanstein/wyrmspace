Wyrmfall

A D&D 3.5 campaign in the dragon shell of Wyrmspace

Beyond the familiar spheres, across the churning phlogiston, lies an older and stranger crystal shell: Wyrmspace. It has two suns: Solthar, the Radiant, and Umbrathis, the Dim. A river of living silver-gold light, the Wyrmwake, threads between its worlds. Dragons rule it, loosely and mostly by argument.

It began with a dream: a friend dragged into the dark by something vast and shadowed.
